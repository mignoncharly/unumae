# App Review notes

Copy the note below into App Store Connect after the owner has confirmed the
support contact and the exact release build. The note is intentionally written
for guest review first; a reviewer does not need an account to inspect the
public experience.

```text
Unumae is fully usable as a guest. No account is required to read Today's Human, the complete portrait, questions and answers, or the Human Archive.

Account deletion is available in Settings -> Delete my account. This removes the account and its profile data, photographs, questions and private library; it is not only a deactivation.

The daily Human may not be live when you review the app. A Quiet Day is intended, honest behaviour: if nobody has accepted and passed review in time, Unumae does not invent a person or publish unreviewed work. The Archive may also be empty before the first publication.

The daily draw is not a sweepstake or contest. There is no entry fee, prize or purchasable advantage.

User-generated content is moderated before publication. A human reviews every portrait, and questions remain pending until approved. Report and block are available on every published portrait and question; there is no direct messaging or comment thread. Community rules must be accepted in-app before participation. A moderation appeal is reviewed by a different moderator from the original decision.

Sign in with Apple is available. The other sign-in path uses an email six-digit code and no password; a reviewer may test it with an email address they control. Participation features require an account, but guest reading does not.

The app is iPhone-only at launch, free, has no in-app purchases or advertising, and uses one global 24-hour UTC cycle. The Privacy Policy, Terms, Community Guidelines and selection explanation are linked from the public website and the app.
```

## App Store Connect fields that remain owner-owned

- App Review contact name, phone number and email must be the staffed support
  contact, not a developer placeholder.
- If the reviewer needs a seeded published Human, provide a representative
  review state from the exact submitted build and state its expiry honestly.
- Confirm the final legal controller identity, support response target and
  escalation route before publishing the URLs.
- Attachments are unnecessary for the normal review path. Add only a short
  reviewer-facing reproduction note if a provider or hosted-state dependency
  cannot be exercised from the guest path.
