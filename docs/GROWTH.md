# Growth

Phase 15. What we are allowed to do to get people here, and what we are not.

Nothing in this document is permitted before `growth_gate()` passes. See
`docs/BETA.md`: if D1 is bad, we buy no users and fix the product instead.
These are the experiments for when it is good.

---

## The one mechanism

Article 1.8: a person passing on somebody they found worth passing on. That is
the only growth mechanism the product has, and the only one it is allowed.

Everything below is a way of putting one stranger in front of people who have
never heard of us. None of it is a way of making existing users bring others —
there are no invites, no referral rewards, no "share to unlock", and there will
not be. A reward for sharing turns a person you found moving into a coupon.

---

## The hooks

Four platforms, four different reasons a stranger stops scrolling.

### TikTok

> **Today, 12,000 people met this one stranger.**

The strongest line we have, because it inverts the thing every other platform
sells. Not "this creator has 12,000 followers" — the 12,000 belong to nobody.
They are the audience, not the person's property, and tomorrow they will be
somebody else's.

**The constraint that comes with it:** that number is a fact about the product,
never a number attached to a person inside the app. `analytics_events` can tell
us how many installs saw a given day. It must never appear on a portrait, in
the Archive, or in a share card. A campaign can say "12,000 people met one
stranger yesterday". The app must never say "12,000 people saw you" — that is a
view count, and a view count is a score with better manners.

If this line is ever used with a real figure, it is generated by hand from the
KPI query for a campaign, not wired into a screen.

### Instagram

The portrait. Nothing else.

A face, a first name, a country, one line in their own words. It is already the
right shape — `ShareCard` renders exactly this at 1080×1350, which is what the
grid wants. There is no caption strategy to invent; the portrait either makes
somebody curious about a stranger or it does not, and that is the same question
the product itself is asking.

### X

A quote from Today's Human.

One line of somebody's answer, attributed to a first name and a country, with
the link. It works for the same reason the portrait works and takes less room:
an ordinary sentence from a person you will never meet is more arresting than
an extraordinary sentence from someone you already follow.

Pick the line the same way the share card does — the first portrait element —
rather than curating "the best" one. Curating is editorial control over which
humans get amplified, which is the beginning of a ranking, and this product does
not rank people.

### Reddit

> **We built a social network where nobody can have followers.**

This is the one that gets argued about, which is the point. It is a claim about
architecture, and the people on Reddit who will pick at it are exactly the
people who can verify it: there is no `followers` column, no `following`, no
`popularity_score`, no `likes_received`, no `engagement_score`, no `reach` —
`scripts/verify-migrations.mjs` rejects any migration that introduces one, and
`src/constants/constitution.ts` lists them.

Bring receipts. The claim survives inspection, and a claim that survives
inspection on Reddit is worth more than any amount of copy.

## What the numbers may say

At 1,000 people the transparency block on "How selection works" reads:

```
1,042 Humans waiting
43 countries
137 languages
```

This is honest marketing and honest product at once, which is rare enough to be
worth protecting. It measures the reader's odds rather than our size. Two rules
keep it that way:

1. **It is never rounded up, softened, or held back when it stops flattering
   us.** One in fifty thousand is a worse sentence than one in a thousand. It
   stays on the page. Article 12 has no exception for numbers that stop
   helping.
2. **Countries with fewer than five people waiting are counted but never
   named** — being the only person waiting somewhere means being identified the
   day you are drawn. The remainder is published alongside, so the arithmetic
   still reconciles.

## What is forbidden

Not "discouraged". These are the things that would make the product a different
product, so they are written down before anyone is under pressure to try them.

| Never | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Referral rewards, invite codes, share-to-unlock | Turns a person you found moving into a coupon |
| Paying for placement in the draw, in any form | Article 5 — the draw takes no inputs but eligibility and chance |
| "X people viewed you" anywhere in the app | A view count is a score with better manners |
| Notifications to bring somebody back | Four categories exist and no more (Phase 10); there is no code path for a fifth |
| Buying users before the gate passes | `docs/BETA.md`. This is the rule the whole of Phase 14 exists to enforce |
| Featuring "the best" Humans | Curation is a ranking with a nicer name |
| Streaks, badges that unlock anything, daily-return rewards | The product is a daily habit or it is nothing; a habit bought with mechanics is not the habit we want |

Founding Humans is the one badge, and it is deliberately worth nothing — see
`docs/BETA.md`.

## Measuring whether it worked

`share_started` and `share_completed`, and the gap between them. That gap is the
only honest measure of whether a portrait was worth passing on: everything up to
`share_started` measures our button, and only the completion measures the
person.

One caveat to read the share rate correctly: the image path uses `expo-sharing`,
which cannot report whether the person went through with it, so it counts
opening the sheet. The text path uses the system share sheet, which can. The
figure is therefore slightly generous when cards are in use. It is written down
here rather than corrected, because a number you understand the bias of is more
useful than one you have quietly adjusted.
