# Website image policy

The Human is the subject of Unumae, not a replaceable visual asset. This policy
applies to every image committed to or loaded by the marketing site.

## People

- A person may be presented as an Unumae Human only with documented consent for
  the exact web use and publication window.
- Stock, generated, commissioned, or demo people are never labelled as Today’s
  Human or placed in a composition that implies they were selected.
- Before consented portraits exist, use non-literal campaign art or clearly
  labelled demonstrations. Quiet Day is always preferable to inventing a Human.
- Removed Human content is removed from web assets and caches. The Archive keeps
  only the approved number-and-date tombstone.

## Rights and provenance

- Accept only original work, commissioned work with a written web licence, or
  assets whose commercial licence has been checked at the time of download.
- Record creator, source URL, licence, licence URL, acquisition date, permitted
  edits, and where the asset is used in `IMAGE_CREDITS.md` before merging it.
- Do not hotlink. Store an optimized source in the repository or approved media
  service and retain the original provenance record.
- AI-assisted abstract art must record the tool, date, prompt summary, and human
  reviewer. It may not imitate a living artist or depict a fake selected person.

## Delivery and accessibility

- Provide meaningful alt text when an image communicates information; use an
  empty alt attribute when it is decorative.
- Set intrinsic width and height, provide responsive sources, and prefer AVIF or
  WebP with a suitable fallback.
- Load the opening visual eagerly only when it is the page’s largest contentful
  element. Lazy-load images below the fold.
- Cropping must not remove context in a way that changes the meaning of a real
  person’s portrait.

## Current state

Phase 2 uses one CSS-built abstract campaign study in the internal component
page. It contains no photograph, generated bitmap, or representation of a real
or fictional Human, so there is no external image credit yet.
